# What sound is {#what-sound-is}

Sound is a pressure wave travelling through air. A source pushes the air near
it back and forth, and the push travels outward at about 343 metres per second.

## Frequency {#sec-frequency}

The number of pushes per second is the frequency, measured in hertz. We hear
it as pitch: the A above middle C is 440 Hz, and each octave doubles it.

## Amplitude {#sec-amplitude}

How far the pressure swings is the amplitude. We hear it as loudness. A
digital recording stores the amplitude many thousands of times per second;
@sec-sampling in the next chapter does exactly that.
