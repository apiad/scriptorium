# Services Agreement

This agreement is made between **ACME Holdings S.A.** (the *Client*),
represented by its director, and **Syalia S.R.L.** (the *Provider*), represented
by its legal representative. Both parties state that they have the capacity to
contract and agree to the following clauses.

::: clauses

## Object

The Provider shall design, build and deliver the document system described in
Annex A, under the terms of this agreement and the applicable law.[@civil]

1. The Provider shall deliver the system no later than 30 November 2026.
2. The Client shall supply the sample documents listed in Annex A within ten
   working days of signature.

## Price and payment

The total price is set out in the table below. The Client shall pay each
instalment within fifteen days of receiving the corresponding invoice.[^vat]

| Instalment | Due on | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| First | Signature | 4 000.00 |
| Second | Delivery | 4 000.00 |
| Total | | 8 000.00 |

[^vat]: Amounts are stated net of value-added tax, which is invoiced separately.

## Confidentiality and data protection

Each party shall keep confidential any information it receives from the other
and process personal data only as the applicable regulation allows.[@gdpr]

## Term and termination

This agreement takes effect on signature and ends when both parties have met
their obligations. Either party may terminate it by written notice if the other
fails to remedy a material breach within thirty days.
